Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

Cue is a private place for the things you want to return to. This policy describes the Cue iPhone app and its Share Extension.

On this page

What Cue keeps

Links, text, images, titles, notes, purposes, timing choices, and text recognized from screenshots are saved on your iPhone. Cue also keeps your reminder preference and the local notifications you choose to schedule. The Share Extension temporarily stages a find in the app’s shared local storage until Cue imports it.

What Cue sends

Cue has no account, Cue server, advertising, analytics SDK, or tracking SDK. Saved finds are not uploaded to us. When a saved web link appears in Queue or Library, Cue may ask the linked website for a title or preview image. That website can receive a normal network request and handle it under its own privacy policy. Opening a link also takes you to the destination website or app.

Photos, text recognition, and reminders

Cue uses only the photo you select or share. Screenshot text recognition runs on your device. If you choose a reminder, Cue asks iOS for notification permission and schedules it locally. You can turn reminders off in Cue’s Settings or change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Keeping and deleting finds

Your finds remain on your device until you remove them. Discarded finds can be restored; Settings lets you permanently delete all discarded finds, including their saved images and notes. Removing Cue from your iPhone removes its local app data, subject to any device backups managed by iOS.

Changes to this policy

If Cue’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe them. This policy covers the iPhone app and Share Extension, not a separate website or a website you open from a saved link.
